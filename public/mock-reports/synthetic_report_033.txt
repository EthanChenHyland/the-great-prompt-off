MRI KNEE WITHOUT CONTRAST

Clinical indication: Medial knee pain after twisting injury.

Technique: Multiplanar MR imaging of the knee was performed without intravenous contrast.

Findings:

Medial compartment:
The medial meniscus is intact. There is mild superficial chondral thinning in the medial femorotibial compartment without focal high-grade cartilage loss. No medial compartment fracture is identified.

Lateral compartment:
The lateral meniscus is intact. Lateral femorotibial cartilage is preserved. No lateral compartment marrow contusion is present.

Femoropatellar joint:
The femoropatellar cartilage is preserved. The quadriceps tendon, patellar tendon, and patellar retinacula are intact.

Cruciate ligaments:
The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact.

Collateral ligaments and posterolateral corner:
The medial collateral ligament is mildly thickened and increased in signal, with edema along both the superficial and deep margins. The ligament remains continuous, in keeping with a low-grade sprain. The lateral collateral ligament complex is intact.

Soft tissues:
There is a moderate joint effusion. A small Baker cyst is present with mild adjacent fluid signal, suggesting low-grade leakage. No acute fracture is seen.

Impression:
1. Low-grade medial collateral ligament sprain without ligament disruption.
2. Moderate joint effusion.
3. Small mildly leaking Baker cyst.
4. No meniscal or cruciate ligament tear.
