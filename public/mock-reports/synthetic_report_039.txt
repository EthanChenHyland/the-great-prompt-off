MRI LEFT KNEE WITHOUT CONTRAST

Clinical indication: Anterior knee pain.

Technique: Multiplanar MR imaging of the left knee was performed without intravenous contrast.

Findings:

Medial compartment:
The medial meniscus is intact. Mild medial femorotibial cartilage thinning is present without focal full-thickness cartilage loss. No medial compartment marrow edema is seen.

Lateral compartment:
The lateral meniscus is intact. Mild lateral femorotibial cartilage thinning is present without focal full-thickness defect. No acute lateral compartment osseous abnormality is present.

Femoropatellar joint:
Femoropatellar osteoarthritis is present, most pronounced at the lateral patellar facet and lateral trochlea, where there is high-grade chondral thinning, subchondral cystic change, and small marginal osteophytes. The extensor mechanism is intact.

Cruciate ligaments:
The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact.

Collateral ligaments and posterolateral corner:
The medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Soft tissues:
There is a small to moderate joint effusion. No Baker cyst is identified. No periarticular fluid collection is seen.

Impression:
1. Femoropatellar osteoarthritis with high-grade lateral femoropatellar chondral loss.
2. Small to moderate joint effusion.
3. No meniscal or ligament tear.
