MRI RIGHT KNEE

CLINICAL HISTORY: Chronic anterior and medial knee pain.

TECHNIQUE: Noncontrast multiplanar MRI of the right knee was obtained.

FINDINGS:
There is no acute fracture or osteonecrosis.

Moderate medial femorotibial osteoarthrosis is present, with diffuse cartilage thinning, small marginal osteophytes, and mild subchondral reactive edema in the medial femoral condyle. The lateral compartment is preserved.

There is also moderate patellofemoral osteoarthrosis. This is most pronounced at the medial patellar facet and central trochlea, where high-grade chondral fissuring and thinning are present.

The medial and lateral menisci are intact. The ACL, PCL, medial collateral ligament, and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact. There is trace joint fluid. No Baker cyst is identified. The extensor mechanism is intact.

IMPRESSION:
1. Moderate medial femorotibial osteoarthrosis.
2. Moderate patellofemoral osteoarthrosis with high-grade chondral fissuring/thinning at the medial patellar facet and central trochlea.
3. No meniscal tear or ligament injury.
