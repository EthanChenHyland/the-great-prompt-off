MRI LEFT KNEE

Clinical indication: Prior partial meniscectomy.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MR imaging of the left knee was performed without intravenous contrast.

Findings:

Medial compartment:
There is expected mild truncation of the medial meniscal body related to prior partial meniscectomy. No displaced meniscal fragment or recurrent surfacing tear is identified. Medial compartment cartilage is preserved. No medial compartment marrow edema is present.

Lateral compartment:
The lateral meniscus is intact. Lateral femorotibial cartilage is maintained. No lateral compartment osseous abnormality is seen.

Femoropatellar joint:
Minimal superficial patellar chondral irregularity is present. The extensor mechanism is intact.

Cruciate ligaments:
The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact.

Collateral ligaments and posterolateral corner:
The medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Soft tissues:
There is no joint effusion or Baker cyst. No soft tissue mass is identified.

Impression:
1. Expected postoperative appearance of partial medial meniscectomy without recurrent tear.
2. No acute internal derangement.
