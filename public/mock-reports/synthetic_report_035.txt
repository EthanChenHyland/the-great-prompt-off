MRI LEFT KNEE

Clinical indication: Recent trauma.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MR imaging of the left knee was performed without intravenous contrast.

Findings:

Medial compartment:
The medial meniscus is intact. Medial femorotibial cartilage is maintained. There is no medial compartment marrow contusion or fracture.

Lateral compartment:
Bone marrow edema is present in the anterolateral femoral condyle and adjacent lateral tibial plateau, compatible with mild osseous contusions. No discrete fracture line, articular surface depression, or osteochondral fragment is identified. The lateral meniscus is intact. Lateral compartment cartilage is preserved.

Femoropatellar joint:
There is minimal superficial patellar chondral irregularity. The extensor mechanism is intact.

Cruciate ligaments:
The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact.

Collateral ligaments and posterolateral corner:
The medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Soft tissues:
There is a small joint effusion. No Baker cyst is identified. No focal soft tissue collection is seen.

Impression:
1. Mild lateral compartment bone contusions without fracture.
2. Small joint effusion.
3. No meniscal, cruciate, or collateral ligament tear.
