MRI RIGHT KNEE

Clinical background:
Anterior knee pain.

Technique:
MRI study of the right knee without intravenous contrast.

Results:
Bone marrow:
No fracture, bone bruise, or suspicious marrow signal abnormality.

Femorotibial compartments:
Medial and lateral menisci have preserved morphology and signal. Femorotibial cartilage is maintained. No Stoller grade 3 meniscal lesion is seen.

Patellofemoral compartment:
Moderate to high-grade chondropathy is present at the medial patellar facet and patellar apex, with minimal reactive subchondral signal. Mild irregularity is also seen at the trochlear groove. Trochlear morphology is shallow.

Ligaments:
The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact. Medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Joint and tendons:
Only a trace amount of physiological joint fluid. Quadriceps and patellar tendons are intact. No Baker cyst.

Conclusion:
Patellofemoral chondropathy/osteoarthrosis, predominant at the medial patellar facet.
Shallow trochlea, which may favor patellar maltracking.
No meniscal or ligamentous lesion.
