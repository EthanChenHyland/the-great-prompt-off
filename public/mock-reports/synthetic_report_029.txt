MRI RIGHT KNEE

Clinical background:
Knee pain.

Technique:
MRI of the right knee without intravenous contrast.

Results:
Osseous structures:
Normal bone marrow signal. No fracture, contusion, osteonecrosis, or focal osteochondral lesion.

Menisci:
Medial and lateral menisci are normal in morphology and signal. No meniscal tear.

Ligaments:
Anterior cruciate ligament, posterior cruciate ligament, medial collateral ligament, and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact and of normal signal.

Cartilage:
Cartilage of the medial femorotibial, lateral femorotibial, and patellofemoral compartments is preserved.

Joint and soft tissues:
No joint effusion. No Baker cyst. Quadriceps tendon, patellar tendon, and periarticular soft tissues are unremarkable.

Conclusion:
Normal MRI examination of the right knee.
