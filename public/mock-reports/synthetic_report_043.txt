LEFT KNEE MRI

HISTORY: Medial knee pain following twisting injury.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar MRI of the left knee was performed without gadolinium.

FINDINGS:
The proximal medial collateral ligament is mildly thickened and edematous, with a small amount of adjacent soft tissue edema. The ligament remains continuous, without retraction or complete tear. The lateral collateral ligament complex is intact.

The ACL and PCL are intact. The medial and lateral menisci are intact. Articular cartilage is preserved in the medial, lateral, and patellofemoral compartments.

Bone marrow signal is normal. There is no fracture or marrow contusion. There is no joint effusion or Baker cyst. The extensor mechanism is intact.

IMPRESSION:
1. Low-grade sprain of the proximal MCL.
2. No meniscal tear, cruciate ligament injury, or acute osseous abnormality.
