RIGHT KNEE MRI

CLINICAL HISTORY: Acute knee injury with instability.

TECHNIQUE: MRI of the right knee was obtained without IV contrast.

FINDINGS:
There is a complete tear of the anterior cruciate ligament with fiber discontinuity and edema in the intercondylar notch. The posterior cruciate ligament is intact.

Typical pivot-shift marrow contusions are present at the lateral femoral condyle and posterolateral tibial plateau. There is also mild contusion at the posterior medial tibial plateau. No depressed osteochondral fracture is identified.

There is a vertical longitudinal tear of the posterior horn of the medial meniscus reaching the inferior articular surface. A radial tear is also present at the posterior horn/body junction of the lateral meniscus. No displaced bucket-handle fragment is present.

The medial collateral ligament is intact, with mild bowing. The lateral collateral ligament complex and posterolateral corner structures are intact. Articular cartilage is preserved without a focal high-grade defect. There is a moderate effusion. The extensor mechanism is intact.

IMPRESSION:
1. Complete ACL tear.
2. Pivot-shift bone contusions involving the lateral femoral condyle and posterolateral tibial plateau, with mild posterior medial tibial plateau contusion.
3. Medial and lateral meniscal tears as above.
4. Moderate knee joint effusion.
