RIGHT KNEE MRI WITHOUT CONTRAST

CLINICAL INFORMATION: Knee swelling and posterior fullness.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar noncontrast MR images of the right knee were obtained.

FINDINGS:
There is a moderate joint effusion with diffuse synovial thickening. A moderate Baker cyst is present in the popliteal fossa and contains mild internal debris. No loose body is identified.

The medial and lateral menisci are intact. The ACL, PCL, medial collateral ligament, and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

No acute fracture, bone contusion, or osteonecrosis is present. Cartilage is preserved in the medial and lateral femorotibial compartments. Mild superficial chondral irregularity is present at the patella, without a focal high-grade defect. The extensor mechanism is intact.

IMPRESSION:
1. Moderate knee joint effusion with synovitis.
2. Moderate Baker cyst with mild internal debris.
3. No meniscal tear, ligament injury, or acute osseous abnormality.
