MRI RIGHT KNEE WITHOUT CONTRAST

Clinical indication: Acute injury with instability.

Technique: Multiplanar MR imaging of the right knee was obtained without intravenous contrast.

Findings:

Medial compartment:
The medial meniscus is intact. Mild diffuse chondral thinning is present without a focal full-thickness cartilage defect. No medial compartment fracture is seen.

Lateral compartment:
There is an acute nondisplaced fracture of the posterior lateral tibial plateau extending to the subchondral bone plate, with surrounding marrow edema. Additional osseous contusion is present in the lateral femoral condyle. A small peripheral vertical tear involves the posterior horn of the lateral meniscus.

Femoropatellar joint:
Mild diffuse chondral thinning is present without focal high-grade femoropatellar cartilage loss. The extensor mechanism is intact.

Cruciate ligaments:
The anterior cruciate ligament is torn proximally, with wavy discontinuous fibers in the intercondylar notch. The posterior cruciate ligament is intact.

Collateral ligaments and posterolateral corner:
Mild edema is present about the proximal medial collateral ligament, consistent with a grade 1 sprain. The lateral collateral ligament complex is intact.

Soft tissues:
There is a moderate joint effusion with mild synovitis. No Baker cyst or periarticular soft tissue mass is identified.

Impression:
1. Acute proximal ACL tear.
2. Nondisplaced posterior lateral tibial plateau fracture with lateral femoral condyle contusion.
3. Small peripheral vertical tear of the posterior horn lateral meniscus.
4. Grade 1 MCL sprain.
5. Moderate joint effusion.
