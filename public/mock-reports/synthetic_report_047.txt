LEFT KNEE MRI

HISTORY: Lateral knee pain.

TECHNIQUE: Routine MRI of the left knee was performed without contrast.

FINDINGS:
There is a horizontal tear of the body and anterior horn of the lateral meniscus extending to the superior articular surface. No displaced meniscal fragment is identified. The medial meniscus is intact.

The ACL, PCL, MCL, and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact. Cartilage thickness is maintained in the medial, lateral, and patellofemoral compartments.

No fracture or marrow contusion is seen. There is a small to moderate joint effusion. No Baker cyst is present. The quadriceps and patellar tendons are intact.

IMPRESSION:
1. Horizontal tear of the body and anterior horn of the lateral meniscus.
2. Small to moderate joint effusion.
3. No ligament tear or acute osseous abnormality.
