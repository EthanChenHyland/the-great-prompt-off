MRI LEFT KNEE

Clinical indication: Knee pain.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MR imaging of the left knee was obtained without intravenous contrast.

Findings:

Medial compartment:
The medial meniscus is normal in morphology without a surfacing tear. Medial femorotibial cartilage thickness is preserved. There is no marrow edema or subchondral fracture in the medial compartment.

Lateral compartment:
The lateral meniscus is intact. Lateral compartment cartilage is maintained. No lateral compartment osseous contusion is present.

Femoropatellar joint:
There is minimal superficial irregularity of the patellar cartilage without a high-grade chondral defect. The trochlear cartilage is preserved. The quadriceps and patellar tendons are intact.

Cruciate ligaments:
The anterior cruciate ligament is thickened and increased in internal signal, with small intraligamentous cystic change, compatible with mucoid/cystic degeneration. There is no fiber discontinuity or secondary MR evidence of an acute ACL tear. The posterior cruciate ligament is intact.

Collateral ligaments and posterolateral corner:
The medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Soft tissues:
There is no significant joint effusion or popliteal cyst. The surrounding soft tissues are unremarkable.

Impression:
1. Mucoid/cystic degeneration of the ACL without tear.
2. No meniscal tear or acute osseous abnormality.
