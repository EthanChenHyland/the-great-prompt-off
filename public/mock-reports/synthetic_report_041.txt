LEFT KNEE MRI

HISTORY: Medial knee pain. Question meniscal tear.

TECHNIQUE: Routine multiplanar MRI of the left knee was obtained without IV contrast.

FINDINGS:
There is no acute fracture or osteonecrosis. Alignment is maintained.

There is an oblique undersurface tear of the posterior horn extending into the posterior body of the medial meniscus. No displaced flap or bucket-handle fragment is present. The lateral meniscus is intact.

The ACL, PCL, medial collateral ligament, and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact. Moderate medial compartment degenerative change is present, with chondral thinning, small marginal spurs, and mild reactive edema in the medial tibial plateau. The lateral compartment cartilage is preserved. Patellofemoral cartilage is without a high-grade defect.

There is a small knee joint effusion. A small Baker cyst is present. The extensor mechanism is intact.

IMPRESSION:
1. Oblique tear of the posterior horn/body of the medial meniscus.
2. Moderate medial compartment osteoarthrosis with mild reactive subchondral edema.
3. Small joint effusion and small Baker cyst.
