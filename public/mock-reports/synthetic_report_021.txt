MRI RIGHT KNEE

Clinical background:
Recent rotational trauma of the knee with swelling.

Technique:
MRI of the right knee was performed without intravenous contrast, in three planes, using standard proton density and fluid-sensitive sequences.

Results:
Osseous structures:
Bone contusion pattern is seen at the lateral femoral condyle and posterior aspect of the lateral tibial plateau, compatible with pivot-shift trauma. No depressed osteochondral fracture is demonstrated. Mild edema is also present in the posterior medial tibial plateau.

Femorotibial compartments:
The medial and lateral menisci are preserved in morphology. No meniscal tear or displaced fragment is seen. The femorotibial cartilage is maintained, without focal high-grade chondropathy.

Patellofemoral compartment:
The patellar and trochlear cartilage surfaces are preserved. The extensor apparatus is continuous.

Ligaments:
Complete rupture of the anterior cruciate ligament, with interruption of the fibers and edema in the intercondylar notch. The posterior cruciate ligament is intact. The medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Joint and soft tissues:
Moderate joint effusion. No popliteal cyst.

Conclusion:
Complete tear of the ACL with pivot-shift type osseous contusions.
Moderate joint effusion.
No associated meniscal tear or collateral ligament rupture detected.
