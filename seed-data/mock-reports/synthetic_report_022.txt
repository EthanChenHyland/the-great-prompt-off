MRI LEFT KNEE

Clinical background:
Chronic medial knee pain.

Technique:
Multiplanar MRI of the left knee was obtained without contrast.

Results:
Osseous structures:
No recent fracture or bone contusion. Mild reactive subchondral edema is present on the medial side, related to the chondral disease.

Medial compartment:
Moderate femorotibial osteoarthrosis with chondropathy predominating on the weight-bearing medial femoral condyle. There is diffuse cartilage thinning, small marginal osteophytes, and mild subchondral reaction. The medial meniscus is intact, without surfacing tear.

Lateral compartment:
The lateral meniscus is normal. Lateral compartment cartilage is preserved.

Patellofemoral compartment:
No significant patellar or trochlear chondral defect is identified.

Ligaments and tendons:
ACL, PCL, MCL, and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact. Extensor mechanism is preserved.

Joint:
No significant joint effusion. No Baker cyst.

Conclusion:
Moderate medial femorotibial osteoarthritis.
No meniscal tear or ligament injury.
