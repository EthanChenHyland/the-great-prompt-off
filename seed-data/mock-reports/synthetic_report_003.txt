EXAM: MRI right knee without contrast

INDICATION: Lateral knee pain and mechanical symptoms.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the right knee was performed without IV contrast.

FINDINGS:
Menisci: Horizontal tear of the lateral meniscal body extending into the anterior horn and contacting the superior articular surface. Mild peripheral extrusion of the lateral meniscal body. The medial meniscus is intact.

Ligaments: ACL and PCL are intact. MCL and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Cartilage and bone: Mild focal chondral irregularity at the lateral tibial plateau. Medial and patellofemoral cartilage are otherwise maintained. No acute osseous abnormality.

Extensor mechanism/soft tissues: Extensor mechanism is intact. Small joint effusion.

IMPRESSION:
1. Horizontal tear of the body/anterior horn of the lateral meniscus with mild extrusion.
2. Mild lateral compartment chondral irregularity and small joint effusion.
