MRI LEFT KNEE

Technique: Multiplanar MRI left knee without contrast.

Findings:
Bone: no acute fracture.
Medial meniscus: oblique inferior surface tear at posterior horn.
Lateral meniscus: intact.
ACL: intact.
PCL: intact.
MCL: proximal thickening and periligamentous edema, low-grade sprain. No complete tear.
LCL complex: intact.
Cartilage: mild medial chondral thinning. Lateral and patellofemoral cartilage maintained.
Joint: small effusion.
Extensor mechanism: intact.

Conclusion:
1. Low-grade proximal MCL sprain.
2. Oblique posterior horn medial meniscus tear.
3. Small joint effusion.
4. No cruciate ligament tear.
