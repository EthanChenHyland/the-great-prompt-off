MRI RIGHT KNEE

Clinical indication: Knee pain with mechanical symptoms.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MR imaging of the right knee was performed without intravenous contrast.

Findings:

Medial compartment:
There is an oblique undersurface tear involving the posterior horn and body of the medial meniscus, with mild peripheral extrusion of the body segment. Moderate chondral thinning is present in the weightbearing medial femorotibial compartment, accompanied by mild reactive subchondral marrow edema at the medial tibial plateau. No acute fracture is identified.

Lateral compartment:
There is a small radial tear/fraying abnormality at the free edge of the lateral meniscal body. Mild superficial chondral irregularity is present without a focal full-thickness cartilage defect.

Femoropatellar joint:
Mild superficial chondral irregularity is present. Patellar alignment is maintained. The extensor mechanism is intact.

Cruciate ligaments:
The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact.

Collateral ligaments and posterolateral corner:
The medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Soft tissues:
There is a small joint effusion. No Baker cyst is present. No periarticular soft tissue mass is seen.

Impression:
1. Oblique undersurface tear of the posterior horn/body of the medial meniscus with mild extrusion.
2. Small radial free-edge tear of the lateral meniscal body.
3. Moderate medial compartment chondrosis with mild reactive subchondral edema.
4. Small joint effusion.
