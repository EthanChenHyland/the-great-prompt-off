MRI RIGHT KNEE

Clinical background:
Lateral knee pain after fall.

Technique:
MRI examination of the right knee without contrast, multiplanar protocol.

Results:
Osseous structures:
Mild bone marrow edema is present in the lateral femoral condyle and anterolateral tibial plateau, in favor of osseous contusions. No visible fracture line or articular depression.

Lateral compartment:
Horizontal tear of the body and anterior horn of the lateral meniscus, extending to the superior articular surface. No displaced meniscal fragment. Lateral compartment cartilage is preserved.

Medial compartment:
Medial meniscus is intact. Medial compartment cartilage is preserved.

Patellofemoral compartment:
No high-grade patellofemoral chondral defect.

Ligaments and tendons:
ACL, PCL, MCL, and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact. Extensor mechanism is intact.

Joint:
Small joint effusion. No Baker cyst.

Conclusion:
Horizontal tear of the anterior horn/body of the lateral meniscus.
Mild lateral compartment bone contusions, without fracture.
Small joint effusion.
No cruciate or collateral ligament injury.
