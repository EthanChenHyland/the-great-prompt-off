MRI LEFT KNEE

Clinical background:
Medial knee pain after valgus injury.

Technique:
Non-enhanced MRI of the left knee was performed in axial, sagittal, and coronal planes.

Results:
Medial collateral ligament:
Thickening and high signal of the proximal MCL with mild adjacent soft tissue edema. Continuity of the ligament is preserved, in keeping with a grade 1 to low-grade grade 2 sprain.

Other ligaments:
ACL and PCL are intact. The lateral collateral ligament complex is intact.

Osseous structures:
Mild contusion of the lateral femoral condyle. No fracture line and no osteochondral depression.

Menisci and cartilage:
Medial and lateral menisci are intact. No Stoller grade 3 meniscal abnormality. Cartilage of the medial, lateral, and patellofemoral compartments is preserved.

Joint and extensor mechanism:
Small joint effusion. Quadriceps tendon and patellar tendon are intact.

Conclusion:
Low-grade sprain of the proximal MCL.
Mild lateral femoral condyle bone contusion.
Small joint effusion.
No meniscal or cruciate ligament tear.
