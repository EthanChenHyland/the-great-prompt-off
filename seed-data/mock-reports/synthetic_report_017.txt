MRI RIGHT KNEE

Technique: MRI right knee without IV contrast.

Findings:
Bone/cartilage: advanced tricompartmental osteoarthritis. Medial compartment high-grade cartilage loss with subchondral sclerosis/reactive edema. Lateral compartment moderate chondral loss. Patellofemoral compartment high-grade chondral loss, greatest laterally. Marginal osteophytes present.
Medial meniscus: degenerative maceration and extrusion.
Lateral meniscus: degenerative free-edge fraying, no displaced fragment.
ACL/PCL: intact.
Collateral ligaments: intact.
Joint: moderate effusion with synovial thickening.
Baker cyst: small, leaking.
Extensor mechanism: intact.

Conclusion:
1. Advanced tricompartmental osteoarthritis, greatest medial and patellofemoral.
2. Macerated extruded medial meniscus.
3. Moderate effusion/synovitis with small leaking Baker cyst.
4. No acute ligament tear.
