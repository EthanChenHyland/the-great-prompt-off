MRI LEFT KNEE

Technique: Routine MRI left knee, no IV contrast.

Findings:
Bone: no acute fracture. No suspicious marrow lesion.
Medial meniscus: intact.
Lateral meniscus: horizontal superior surface tear involving body and posterior horn, with mild free-edge truncation.
ACL/PCL: intact.
MCL/LCL complex: intact.
Cartilage: moderate lateral femorotibial osteoarthritis with chondral thinning and focal high-grade fissuring at lateral tibial plateau/weightbearing lateral femoral condyle. Medial cartilage preserved. Mild superficial patellofemoral irregularity.
Joint: small effusion. Baker cyst absent.
Extensor mechanism: intact.

Conclusion:
1. Horizontal tear of lateral meniscus body/posterior horn.
2. Moderate lateral compartment osteoarthritis.
3. Small effusion.
4. No cruciate or collateral ligament tear.
