MRI LEFT KNEE

Technique: Noncontrast MRI of the left knee.

Findings:
Bone: focal edema at anterior medial femoral condyle, consistent with contusion. No fracture line. No cortical depression.
Medial meniscus: intact.
Lateral meniscus: intact.
ACL/PCL: intact.
MCL/LCL complex: intact.
Cartilage: preserved, no high-grade defect.
Joint: trace physiologic fluid. No Baker cyst.
Extensor mechanism: intact.

Conclusion:
1. Focal bone contusion of anterior medial femoral condyle.
2. No fracture.
3. No meniscal or ligament injury.
