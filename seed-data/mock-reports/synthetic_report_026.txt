MRI LEFT KNEE

Clinical background:
Medial and anterior knee pain.

Technique:
MRI of the left knee was obtained without intravenous contrast.

Results:
Medial compartment:
Oblique tear of the posterior horn and body of the medial meniscus, reaching the inferior articular surface, corresponding to a Stoller grade 3 lesion. No displaced flap fragment. Minimal superficial chondral thinning is present in the medial compartment.

Lateral compartment:
Lateral meniscus and lateral femorotibial cartilage are preserved.

Patellofemoral compartment:
Moderate chondropathy of the patellar apex and medial patellar facet, with mild subchondral reactive signal. Mild thinning of the trochlear cartilage is also present.

Ligaments:
ACL and PCL are intact. Medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Osseous structures and joint:
No recent fracture or bone contusion. Small joint effusion. Extensor mechanism is continuous.

Conclusion:
Stoller grade 3 oblique tear of the posterior horn/body of the medial meniscus.
Moderate patellofemoral chondropathy.
Small joint effusion.
No cruciate or collateral ligament tear.
