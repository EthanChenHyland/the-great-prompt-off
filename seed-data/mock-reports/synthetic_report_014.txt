MRI LEFT KNEE

Technique: MRI left knee, no contrast.

Findings:
Bone: no fracture.
Medial meniscus: complex tear at posterior horn and body, inferior surface extension.
Lateral meniscus: horizontal tear at body/anterior horn to free edge.
ACL/PCL: intact.
MCL/LCL complex: intact.
Cartilage: mild wear in medial, lateral, and patellofemoral compartments.
Joint: moderate effusion. Mild synovial thickening.
Baker cyst: no sizable cyst.
Extensor mechanism: intact.

Conclusion:
1. Medial and lateral meniscal tears.
2. Moderate effusion with mild synovitis.
3. Mild tricompartmental chondral wear.
4. Cruciate and collateral ligaments intact.
