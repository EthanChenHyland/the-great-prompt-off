MRI LEFT KNEE

Clinical background:
Pain after injury, with chronic medial knee pain.

Technique:
MRI of the left knee was performed without contrast.

Results:
Osseous structures:
Nondisplaced subchondral fracture of the medial tibial plateau with surrounding marrow edema. The articular surface remains preserved, without depression. Mild additional reactive subchondral signal is seen in the medial femoral condyle related to degenerative change.

Medial compartment:
Moderate medial femorotibial osteoarthrosis with diffuse chondral thinning of the weight-bearing surfaces and small osteophytes. Mild degenerative intrasubstance signal of the posterior horn of the medial meniscus, without contact with an articular surface and without Stoller grade 3 tear.

Lateral compartment:
Lateral meniscus and lateral compartment cartilage are preserved.

Patellofemoral compartment:
Mild superficial patellar chondropathy. No high-grade defect.

Ligaments:
ACL, PCL, MCL, and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Joint and tendons:
Small joint effusion. No Baker cyst. Extensor mechanism is intact.

Conclusion:
Nondisplaced subchondral fracture of the medial tibial plateau.
Moderate medial femorotibial osteoarthritis.
Mild degenerative signal of the medial meniscus, without surfacing tear.
No ligament injury.
