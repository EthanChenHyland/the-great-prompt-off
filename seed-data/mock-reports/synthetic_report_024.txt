MRI LEFT KNEE

Clinical background:
Swelling and stiffness of the knee.

Technique:
Non-contrast MRI of the left knee, performed with multiplanar sequences.

Results:
Joint:
Moderate intra-articular effusion with mild diffuse synovial thickening. No loose body is identified. No Baker cyst is present.

Osseous structures:
Normal bone marrow signal. No fracture, contusion, osteonecrosis, or focal osseous lesion.

Menisci:
The medial and lateral menisci are normal in shape, without Stoller grade 3 tear.

Ligaments:
ACL, PCL, MCL, and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Cartilage:
Cartilage of the medial femorotibial, lateral femorotibial, and patellofemoral compartments is preserved. No focal high-grade chondropathy is seen.

Extensor mechanism:
Quadriceps and patellar tendons are intact.

Conclusion:
Moderate knee joint effusion with mild synovitis.
No meniscal tear, ligament injury, or focal cartilage lesion.
