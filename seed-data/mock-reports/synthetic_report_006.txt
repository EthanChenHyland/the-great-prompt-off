EXAM: MRI left knee without contrast

INDICATION: Chronic medial and anterior knee pain.

TECHNIQUE: Multiplanar, multisequence MRI of the left knee was performed without IV contrast.

FINDINGS:
Cartilage and bone: Moderate medial femorotibial osteoarthritis with diffuse chondral thinning, focal high-grade cartilage loss at the weightbearing medial femoral condyle, small marginal osteophytes, and mild reactive subchondral edema. Moderate patellofemoral chondrosis/osteoarthritis is also present, greatest at the medial patellar facet and central trochlea. Lateral compartment cartilage is relatively preserved. No acute fracture.

Menisci: Degenerative intrasubstance signal in the medial meniscus without a discrete surfacing tear. Lateral meniscus is intact.

Ligaments: Cruciate and collateral ligaments are intact.

Extensor mechanism/soft tissues: Small joint effusion. Extensor mechanism is intact.

IMPRESSION:
1. Moderate medial compartment osteoarthritis with focal high-grade chondral loss.
2. Moderate patellofemoral chondrosis/osteoarthritis.
3. No discrete meniscal or ligament tear.
