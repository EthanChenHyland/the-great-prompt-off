MRI RIGHT KNEE

Clinical indication: Medial knee pain and swelling.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MR imaging of the right knee was performed without intravenous contrast.

Findings:

Medial compartment:
Moderate medial femorotibial osteoarthritis is present, with high-grade chondral thinning of the weightbearing medial femoral condyle and medial tibial plateau, marginal osteophytes, and mild reactive subchondral marrow edema. The medial meniscus shows degenerative fraying of the body and posterior horn without a displaced tear.

Lateral compartment:
The lateral meniscus is intact. Lateral femorotibial cartilage is relatively preserved. No lateral compartment fracture or marrow contusion is identified.

Femoropatellar joint:
Mild femoropatellar chondral wear is present without focal full-thickness defect. The extensor mechanism is intact.

Cruciate ligaments:
The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact.

Collateral ligaments and posterolateral corner:
The medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Soft tissues:
There is a moderate joint effusion with synovial thickening. A moderate Baker cyst is present. No loose body is identified.

Impression:
1. Moderate medial compartment osteoarthritis with high-grade chondral loss and mild reactive marrow edema.
2. Degenerative fraying of the medial meniscus without displaced tear.
3. Moderate effusion with synovitis.
4. Moderate Baker cyst.
