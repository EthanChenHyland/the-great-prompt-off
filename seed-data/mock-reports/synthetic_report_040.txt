MRI RIGHT KNEE

Clinical indication: Knee pain.

Technique: Multiplanar, multisequence MR imaging of the right knee was performed without intravenous contrast.

Findings:

Medial compartment:
The medial meniscus is intact. Medial femorotibial cartilage is preserved. There is no medial compartment marrow edema, fracture, or osteochondral lesion.

Lateral compartment:
The lateral meniscus is intact. Lateral femorotibial cartilage is preserved. There is no lateral compartment marrow contusion or fracture.

Femoropatellar joint:
The patellar and trochlear cartilage are preserved. The quadriceps and patellar tendons are intact.

Cruciate ligaments:
The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact.

Collateral ligaments and posterolateral corner:
The medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are intact.

Soft tissues:
There is no joint effusion or Baker cyst. The regional soft tissues are unremarkable.

Impression:
Normal MRI of the right knee.
