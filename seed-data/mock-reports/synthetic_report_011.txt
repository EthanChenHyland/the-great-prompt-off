MRI RIGHT KNEE

Technique: MRI right knee without contrast.

Findings:
Bone: no fracture or marrow contusion.
Medial meniscus: oblique inferior surface tear at posterior horn and body. No displaced fragment.
Lateral meniscus: intact.
ACL/PCL: intact.
MCL/LCL complex: intact.
Cartilage: mild thinning in the medial femorotibial compartment. Lateral and patellofemoral cartilage preserved.
Joint: small effusion. Small Baker cyst.
Extensor mechanism: intact.

Conclusion:
1. Medial meniscus posterior horn/body tear.
2. Mild medial compartment chondral thinning.
3. Small effusion and small Baker cyst.
