MRI RIGHT KNEE WITHOUT CONTRAST

CLINICAL INFORMATION: Chronic lateral knee pain.

TECHNIQUE: Noncontrast multiplanar MR imaging of the right knee was performed.

FINDINGS:
No fracture, bone contusion, or marrow replacing process is identified.

The menisci are normal in morphology without a surfacing tear. The anterior and posterior cruciate ligaments are intact. The medial collateral ligament and lateral collateral ligament complex are also intact.

There is moderate lateral femorotibial osteoarthrosis, with cartilage loss most evident at the weightbearing lateral femoral condyle and lateral tibial plateau. Small marginal osteophytes and minimal subchondral reactive edema are present. The medial femorotibial and patellofemoral cartilage surfaces are maintained.

There is no significant effusion. No popliteal cyst is seen. The quadriceps and patellar tendons are intact.

IMPRESSION:
1. Moderate isolated lateral compartment osteoarthrosis.
2. No meniscal tear or ligament injury.
