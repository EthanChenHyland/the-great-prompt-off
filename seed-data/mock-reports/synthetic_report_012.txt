MRI LEFT KNEE

Technique: Multiplanar MRI left knee without IV contrast.

Findings:
Bone: no acute osseous lesion. Mild reactive signal at the lateral tibial plateau.
Medial meniscus: intact.
Lateral meniscus: mild free-edge fraying, no displaced tear.
ACL/PCL: normal.
Collateral ligaments: normal.
Medial compartment: cartilage relatively maintained.
Lateral compartment: moderate cartilage loss with small marginal osteophytes.
Patellofemoral compartment: high-grade chondral fissuring/thinning at the lateral patellar facet and trochlea.
Joint fluid: trace. Baker cyst: absent.
Extensor mechanism: preserved.

Conclusion:
1. Moderate lateral femorotibial osteoarthritis.
2. Patellofemoral osteoarthritis, high-grade laterally.
3. No ligament injury. No displaced meniscal tear.
