MRI RIGHT KNEE

Clinical background:
Posterior fullness of the knee with intermittent pain.

Technique:
Routine MRI of the right knee was performed without contrast.

Results:
Joint and popliteal region:
Small joint effusion. Small Baker cyst in the medial popliteal fossa, without signs of rupture or extensive leakage.

Osseous structures:
No fracture, bone contusion, or osteonecrosis.

Femorotibial compartments:
The medial and lateral menisci are intact. Cartilage thickness is maintained in the medial and lateral compartments.

Patellofemoral compartment:
Minimal superficial patellar chondropathy. No full-thickness cartilage defect.

Ligaments and tendons:
Cruciate and collateral ligaments are intact. Extensor mechanism is normal.

Conclusion:
Small Baker cyst associated with small joint effusion.
Minimal patellar chondral irregularity.
No meniscal or ligamentous tear.
